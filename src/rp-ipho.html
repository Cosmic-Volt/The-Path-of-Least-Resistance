Reading Pattern — The Path to Monarch

1
Watch MIT OCW Lecture — For each HRK chapter, watch the corresponding MIT OCW lecture first. Build intuition before diving into the book.
↓
2
Read HRK Chapter — Halliday, Resnick, Krane 5th Edition. Every IPhO gold medalist starts here. Days 1–60.
↓
3
Solve HRK Problems — Do the starred problems. They mirror olympiad difficulty.
↓
4
After HRK Complete (Day 60) → Kevin Zhou Handouts — Transition to Kevin Zhou's 1,000+ olympiad problems. This is where you become a hunter.
↓
5
IPhO Past Papers — The final test. Solve under timed conditions. Review every mistake. Become the Monarch.
